Set up your letterbox

Barua will create private letter keys in this browser. Your account password will unlock them automatically after you sign in, so opening an envelope will not require another passphrase.

One simple daily experience

Your account password signs you into Barua and unlocks your private letterbox. Barua never stores that password as a letter key; your browser derives an encryption key from it locally.

Your recovery phrase is an offline backup for your letterbox. It is for a forgotten password or an emergency recovery, not for opening ordinary envelopes.

Save your recovery phrase.
